**Input**: Design documents from `/specs/007-payments-and-access/`

**Prerequisites**: plan.md, spec.md, research.md, data-model.md, contracts/, quickstart.md

- **[Story]**: US1 to US5 from spec.md

**Before writing any Next.js code**: read the relevant guide in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` (AGENTS.md): at least route handlers (`01-app/01-getting-started/15-route-handlers.md`), proxy and Cache Components with runtime data. Use design tokens from `src/app/globals.css` only; coral is a fill with ink labels and `coral-text` for coral text. **Stripe test-mode keys only**; never commit a key. Where a plan or research item says **Verify**, check the installed `stripe` SDK types or the Mux docs before relying on the field.

## Implementation strategy

- **MVP**: Setup, Foundational and US1 (the enforced access rule, the calm locked state and signed playback). It is valuable on its own and safe to ship, since nothing can charge while prices are placeholders. Signed playback needs the Mux signing key and signed-policy uploads before full-access videos can play in production; the launch checklist in quickstart.md covers this.
- **Then**: US2 with US3 together, since paying without the return and webhook loop would leave a learner locked. Then US4, then US5 to remove the last hand-typed price and add drift checking.
- **Launch is not part of these tasks**: real amounts, Stripe Tax registration, Mux signed uploads, live keys and `BILLING_LIVE=true` follow the launch checklist in quickstart.md.
